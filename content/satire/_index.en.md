This section contains serious criticism combined with satirical elements. The texts address philosophical theories, arguments, and books, presenting their weaknesses in a deliberately exaggerated and entertaining form.

The style is partly inspired by Douglas Adams. The humorous presentation is intended to illustrate the arguments and make them more accessible. The underlying philosophical criticism remains serious.
